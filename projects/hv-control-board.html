HV Control Board (Ultimate HV Board)

2026–27 Formula SAE EV · Validation in progress

HV control and safety board that handles precharge, solid-state discharge, accumulator isolation relay (AIR) control, HV-present detection, welded AIR detection, tractive system measurement points (TSMPs), and the logic for the indicator light and RTM safety lights. Sits inside the accumulator container in a seperate section from battery cells in a 3-PCB stackup.
