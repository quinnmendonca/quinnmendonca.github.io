Point-Mass EV Lap Simulation

MATLAB point-mass vehicle model developed for accumulator sizing and performance analysis. Simulates vehicle dynamics, motor limits, battery voltage and current, energy consumption, SOC, and cell temperature over a Formula SAE endurance event.

Used to compare accumulator configurations and select the 114s6p pack architecture for the 2026–27 vehicle.
