HV Control Board (Ultimate HV Board)

4-layer control board for our 478.8 V accumulator. It handles precharge, discharge, HV-present detection, AIR weld detection, relay control, and other accumulator electronics.

Currently testing the 2026–27 revision, picture shown is the test version.

HV Isolated Backplane PCB

Backplane PCB for the accumulator electronics stack. Distributes low-voltage power and routes control and communication signals between the HV Control Board, BMS, and accumulator interfaces while remaining isolated from tractive-system voltage.

Designed to replace point-to-point wiring between accumulator electronics with a defined board-to-board interface.

HV Systems Integration

Electrical architecture and integration for our 114s6p accumulator service section, including HV/LV distribution, harnessing, component layout, and packaging.

Point-Mass EV Lap Simulation

MATLAB point-mass vehicle model developed for accumulator sizing and performance analysis. Simulates vehicle dynamics, motor torque limits, battery current and voltage sag, energy consumption, and cell temperature over an endurance event.

Used to compare accumulator configurations and select the 114s6p pack architecture for the 2026–27 vehicle.
