01 — OVERVIEW

Consolidating the accumulator's high-voltage control and safety functions.

The HV Control Board is a four-layer PCB developed for Bruin Formula Racing's Formula SAE electric vehicle. The board integrates several high-voltage control, monitoring, and safety functions within the accumulator.

The current revision integrates inverter precharge and discharge control, HV-present indication, accumulator isolation relay weld detection, relay control and monitoring, and tractive-system measurement circuitry.

02 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Board functions

Precharge

Controls charging of the inverter DC-link capacitance before the main isolation relays close.

Discharge

Provides controlled discharge of the tractive system after shutdown.

HV Present

Detects tractive-system voltage for vehicle indication and monitoring.

AIR Weld Detection

Monitors the accumulator isolation relays for welded-contact conditions.
